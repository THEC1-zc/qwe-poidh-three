Contour

contour.seer.pm — Market-estimated probability contours across DeFi protocols on matched quarterly horizons.

Concise Naming Rationale

On a map, contours connect points with the same measured value so an observer can read relative steepness across an entire terrain at a glance. That is the exact job of risk.seer.pm (currently titled Foresight | Kleros).

A single yes-or-no market price in isolation tells a DeFi participant very little. By placing DeFi assets side by side on matched quarterly horizons (Q3 2026, Q4 2026) and allowing traders to deploy the same capital across multiple protocols simultaneously, Seer turns isolated binary bets into a comparative risk topography.

  • Honest about what the numbers are: Unlike names that imply insurance, protection, or guaranteed solvency, Contour names the shape of market-implied Probability of Default (PD) estimates. Estimates move as traders reprice exposure; they are a comparative market signal, never a security audit or safety guarantee.
  • Fits the Seer & Electric Foundation family: Plain two-syllable English noun alongside Seer, Deep, Futarchy, and Opportunity, with original contour artwork adapted to the kit’s violet accents and neutral surfaces.
