Design Rationale & Electric Foundation (EF) Kit Notes

What We Inspected from the EF Brand Kit & Deliberate Adaptations

1. EF Kit Foundation Inspected

From the official Figma board (node-id=118-16652), we preserved the dual dark/light neutral surface system (dark #101013 / #1A1A1E / #1F1F24; light #F7F7F9 / #FFFFFF; text #EDEDF2 / #A8A8B3), the compact market card grammar, and the signature violet Up / Yes paired with pale-violet Down / No outcome buttons.

2. Honest Token & Artwork Adaptation Notes

On the Figma swatch sheet, the printed primary label (#67676F) conflicts with its vivid violet appearance, and raw font binaries are not bundled for redistribution. We therefore used #7D33FF (dark) / #6C2BD9 (light) as an explicit violet adaptation, lifted muted secondary text to #90909F for contrast, and used a zero-dependency system + tabular-monospace stack. Our topographic contour artwork is our own original visual adaptation for relative risk comparison.

3. Deliberate Departures Across Specialist Apps

  • Shared Top Ecosystem Bar: Connects all six routes and theme state so users recognize one protocol underneath five specialist products.
  • Comparison-First Tables on Dense Screens: While the Explorer preserves the EF market card grid, Deep, Opportunity Markets, and Risk Pricing (Contour) use aligned tabular rows so dozens of outcomes or quarterly protocol PDs can be compared side by side.
  • Normalized Probabilities & Governance Boundaries: The illustrative binary fixture prices sum to 100¢ ($1.00), the simulated Opportunity cohort normalizes to 100%, Futarchy explicitly separates conditional price correlation from causal proof, and human reviewers/DAOs retain final decision authority.
